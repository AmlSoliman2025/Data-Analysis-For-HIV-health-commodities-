# 📦 Supply Chain Analysis Report

This repository contains a data-driven analysis of supply chain logistics performance across vendors, shipment modes, and fulfillment strategies. The goal is to uncover inefficiencies, outliers, and actionable insights to optimize delivery performance and freight costs.

---

## 📊 Notebook Overview

### 🔍 **File**: `Supply_Chain_Analysis_Plan.ipynb`

This Jupyter Notebook includes:
- **Summary Statistics** of numerical and categorical data
- **Time Frame** of the collected data
- **Projects by year and countries** the distribution of projects allover the countries
- **Delivery Delays** exploring its main items
- **Order life cycle** 
- **Vendor performance benchmarking** (cost, delays, efficiency)
- **Shipment and logistics**
- **Outlier shipment detection** with cost per kg analysis
- **Products movement**
- **Fulfillment route comparison** (Direct Drop vs From RDC)
- **Country insights** 
- **Final strategic recommendations and actions**

---

## ✅ Key Findings

- Ocean shipments via **Direct Drop** incur the highest delays (~40 days).
- **Air shipments** to high-risk countries (e.g., Congo DRC, South Sudan) drive extreme freight costs.
- Fulfillment **From RDC** adds noticeable delivery delay versus direct drop.
- **Truck (Direct Drop)** and **Air Charter** are the most time-efficient shipment modes.
- Some vendors exhibit poor cost efficiency and should be reviewed.

---

## 🛠️ Recommended Actions

| Action                                                 | Impact                             |
|--------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Audit high-cost shipments                              | Reduce freight spend by 15–25%     |
| Optimize shipment modes                                | Shorten delivery by 15+ days       |
| Review RDC fulfillment                                 | Cut lead time by 3–7 days          |
| Renegotiate vendor SLAs (Service Level Agreement)      | Improve on-time delivery by 20%    |
| Tailor country strategies                              | Increase cost-effectiveness        |
